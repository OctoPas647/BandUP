BandUp for iPhone / iPad (installable web app)

1. Put this folder online (free options):
   - Netlify Drop: open https://app.netlify.com/drop and drag this whole folder onto the page.
   - Or GitHub Pages / Cloudflare Pages.
2. On your iPhone, open the link in Safari.
3. Tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
4. Open BandUp from the home screen: it runs full screen and works offline.

Progress is saved on the device. It is separate from the Android app and the web version.
